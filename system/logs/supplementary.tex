\section{Reporting protocol}
This document lists the seed-level scores behind every mean~$\pm$~standard deviation reported in the main paper.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Seeds.} The client partition is fixed (\texttt{partition\_seed}$=1$); model initialization and training use three seeds, $s\in\{0,1,2\}$ (columns s0, s1, s2).
  \item \textbf{Score of one seed.} For each client, macro-F1 is computed on its clean held-out test set. The score of one seed is the weighted arithmetic mean of these per-client values (weight $n_k$) over the evaluated clients.
  \item \textbf{Evaluated clients.} Without label noise, all $K=20$ clients are evaluated. With label noise, only the benign (non-corrupted) clients are evaluated; the corrupted clients are excluded (Table~\ref{tab:s-corrupt-ids}).
  \item \textbf{Mean and std.} ``mean'' is the average of the three seed scores and ``std'' is their sample standard deviation,
  $\mathrm{std}=\sqrt{\tfrac{1}{2}\sum_{s=0}^{2}(F_s-\bar F)^2}$.
  For three values in $[0,1]$ with mean $\bar F\ge 2/3$, this std cannot exceed $\sqrt{3}\,(1-\bar F)$.
  \item \textbf{Code.} Partitioning, noise injection, training, and aggregation scripts: \url{https://github.com/huyentrang154/FedLearning-IDS-2026}.
\end{itemize}
